    <div class="eyebrow">THE VISION</div>
    <h1>About the <span class="highlight">Project.</span></h1>

    <p class="intro">
      This project explores the possibilities of movement
      and interaction in virtual reality through a parkour experience.
    </p>

    <h2>The Concept</h2>

    <p>
      The game combines parkour mechanics with imaginative,
      surreal environments. Its creative direction draws inspiration
      from Alice in Wonderland and the visual language of origami.
    </p>

    <h2>The Goal</h2>

    <p>
      To create an engaging VR experience where movement,
      exploration and challenging gameplay come together.
    </p>

    <h2>The Development Process</h2>

    <p>
      This journal documents the entire journey, including
      the design decisions, technical experiments, failed attempts
      and discoveries that shape the final experience.
    </p>
