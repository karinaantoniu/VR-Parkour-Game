ABOUT THE PROJECT

Behind the Game

This project explores the possibilities of movement and interaction in virtual reality through a parkour experience. Inspired by surreal worlds and imaginative environments, the game combines experimentation, movement mechanics and creative level design.

This journal documents the entire process: the ideas, the decisions, the things that work and the things that don't.
